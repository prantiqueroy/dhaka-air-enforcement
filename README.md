# Do Crackdowns Clear the Air?

**Question:** When the government or courts crack down on air pollution in Dhaka, does PM2.5 actually fall, and for how long?

**Data:** Hourly PM2.5 from the U.S. Embassy monitor in Dhaka (2016–2025), hourly weather from Open-Meteo, and a hand-built timeline of enforcement actions.

**Method:** Remove the effect of weather from the pollution data, then compare pollution before and after each enforcement action.

**Status:** Analysis complete (pre-registered main test, Oct 2026). Result: no detectable fall in weather-adjusted PM2.5 after enforcement actions. Paper in progress.

**Pre-registration:** https://osf.io/6bsek (registered 27 Oct 2026, before the main analysis)

**Author:** Prantique Roy, Dhaka
